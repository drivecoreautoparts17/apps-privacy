Block Kingdom – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Block Kingdom - Block Puzzle (package com.covepalms.blockkingdom, "the game") handles information. The game is published on Google Play by its developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or exact location. The game has no accounts and no sign-in.

Your game progress (scores, coins, unlocked themes, settings, daily-challenge streak and a saved game) is stored only on your own device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show ads, including personalised ads where permitted, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded videos

Some features, such as continuing a game or doubling coins, are offered in exchange for watching a video ad. Watching is always optional.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data processed by Google described above, no data from the game is shared with third parties.

Data security and retention

Data processed by Google AdMob is sent over encrypted connections and kept under Google's own retention policies. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
